# Voice Input Usage Guide

## Quick Start

Your PansGPT application now supports voice input! Here's how to use it:

## 🎤 Using Voice Input in Quiz Short Answer Questions

1. **Start a Quiz** with Short Answer questions
2. **Scroll down** to the answer textarea
3. **Click the "Voice Input" button** (🎤 icon with green background)
4. **Speak your answer** clearly into your microphone
5. **Click "Stop Recording"** when finished
6. **Wait a moment** for transcription (you'll see "Transcribing..." status)
7. Your answer will **automatically appear** in the textarea!

## 💬 Using Voice Input in Chat Messages

1. **Type your message normally**, OR
2. **Click the microphone button** (🎤 icon) in the input box
3. **Speak your question** into your microphone
4. **Click the microphone again** to stop recording
5. **Your transcribed text** will appear in the input field
6. **Click "Send"** to send your message

## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

### Microphone Setup
- ✅ Use a good quality microphone
- ✅ Find a quiet environment
- ✅ Speak clearly and at a normal pace
- ✅ Keep consistent distance from microphone

### Pronunciation
- ✅ Speak naturally - don't over-enunciate
- ✅ Avoid background noise
- ✅ Pause briefly between sentences
- ✅ Wait for transcription to complete before speaking again

### Browser Permissions
- When prompted, click **"Allow"** for microphone access
- Check your browser's permission settings if microphone doesn't work

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Microphone Button Not Working?
- Check browser permissions (allow microphone access)
- Try refreshing the page
- Use HTTPS (required for microphone access)
- Check if microphone works in other applications

### Transcription Not Accurate?
- Speak more clearly and slowly
- Reduce background noise
- Check your internet connection
- Try again in a quieter environment

### Recording Indicator Issues?
- Green button = Ready to record
- Red pulsing = Currently recording
- Gray/disabled = System busy (wait a moment)
